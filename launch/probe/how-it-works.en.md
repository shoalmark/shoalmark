The button "Copy the prompt" copies a prompt and opens a window. Paste the prompt into Claude Code or Codex, into the session you already use, and open the page on the machine where your agent runs. The prompt asks your agent to check, honestly, whether shoalmark fits your project, and the answer may well be no. It asks your agent to only read your project, to write only to a throwaway folder outside it, and to change nothing in your project without your yes. It can take up to 45 minutes. Expect your agent to ask you to approve a few things: network access for its downloads from GitHub, running git (on Subversion, svn) and Python, and at the end, only on your yes, opening our feedback form in your browser. That is as intended.
