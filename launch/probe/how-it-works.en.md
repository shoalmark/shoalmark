The button "Copy the prompt" copies a prompt and opens a window. Paste the prompt into Claude Code or Codex, into the session you already use, and open the page on the machine where your agent runs. Your agent checks, honestly, whether shoalmark fits your project, and the answer may well be no. It only reads your project, writes only to a throwaway folder outside it, and changes nothing in your project without your yes. It can take up to 45 minutes. Expect your agent to ask you to approve a few things: network access for the download, and running git and Python. That is as intended.
