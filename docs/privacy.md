## 2. Providing the website

The website is served at shoalmark.tools through Cloudflare Workers by Cloudflare, Inc. Requests to its former address,
shoalmark.github.io/shoalmark, reach GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., which forwards them to shoalmark.tools. To do so,
each provider processes in particular your IP address, HTTP headers and time stamps, to deliver the website, keep it
stable and fend off attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in running the website
securely and reliably.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking services on this website, and load no fonts from third-party CDNs.

## 3. Local display setting

The documentation's pages, this page among them, store the display, light or dark, in your browser's localStorage under
the key `/.__palette`. Each of these pages stores, when it
opens, the display it shows: your choice, if you have switched the display, and otherwise your system's setting. The
setting stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be deleted with your browser data. It serves only the display
you asked for (§ 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG). The start page, "Why shoalmark?" and the feedback page store nothing on your
device.

## 4. Links to GitHub

The pages link to GitHub: shoalmark's repository, "Edit this page" on every page of the documentation, and the feedback
form, to which the page shoalmark.tools/feedback forwards you as soon as it opens. Nothing is sent to GitHub before you
follow one of these links; the link to the repository in the documentation's header, too, asks GitHub for nothing. Once
you follow a link, GitHub's privacy statement applies. The same holds for the other links on these pages, for example to
X or to zensical.org, with the privacy statement of the provider concerned.

## 5. Contact by email

If you write to us by email, we process your address, your message and the data in it, to answer you. The legal basis is
Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your message concerns a contract or its preparation, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our
legitimate interest lies in answering what you send us. We use email service providers to receive and store emails. They
may also process messages in the USA; where they do, the transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on
standard contractual clauses. We delete your messages once your matter is settled, unless statutory retention duties
require us to keep them.

## 6. Recipients and transfer to third countries

The recipients of the delivery data are Cloudflare and GitHub. Cloudflare processes them as a processor. GitHub
processes them as a controller in its own right when it runs GitHub Pages. The transfer to the USA rests on the EU-US
Data Privacy Framework, and for Cloudflare, as we have set it up, also on standard contractual clauses. Recipients of
emails are our email service providers (section 5). There are no other recipients, unless the law obliges us to disclose
data.

## 7. Retention

We keep no web server or analytics logs of our own. Cloudflare and GitHub keep security and delivery data for the
periods their services set. The local display setting stays until you delete it in your browser. Emails are deleted as
section 5 says.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have in particular the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data
portability and objection. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

**Right to object:** where we process data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you may object to it at any time, on
grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We then stop, unless we can show compelling legitimate
grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend
legal claims. Write to [privacy@shoalmark.tools](mailto:privacy@shoalmark.tools).
