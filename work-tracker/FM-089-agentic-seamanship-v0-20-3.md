# FM-089 — Agentic seamanship: v0.20.3

## What is true now

**Filed 2026-10-29; nothing is built.** On the Owner's word of 29 October, as the Auditor relays it: the theme is
*agentic seamanship*, the craft of a team of agents whose records finish, whose evidence stays small, and whose
lessons every seat can read.
- **The theme is never closed.** Every tracker of it carries the tag `seamanship`, which the configuration's tag
  vocabulary holds since the Owner's #229.
- **Each release that carries the theme gets one chapter,** an epic like this one, which ships at that release.
- **This chapter is v0.20.3's.** Its parts:
  - [FM-086](FM-086-a-tracker-states-one-deliverable-and-closes-when-it-ships.md): a tracker states one deliverable
    and closes when it ships, by shoalmark's definition of done;
  - [FM-087](FM-087-evidence-comes-in-three-classes-what-decides-stays-with-the.md): evidence comes in three classes.
- **Its third part:** [FM-088](FM-088-the-team-s-lessons-live-only-in-each-harness-s-local-memory.md), shared memory for
  every seat and for the team, on the Owner's signed answer of 29 October (`febbed9`).
- **Candidates, not parts:** four trackers filed for v0.20.x on the Owner's word of 29 October, "we consider all, but
  we only pull in what fits". v0.20.3's allocation pulls in what fits, and this chapter's Done when doesn't change.
  - [FM-095](FM-095-the-owner-s-rulings-live-in-chat-memories-and-briefs-not-in.md):
    the Owner's rulings in one register;
  - [FM-096](FM-096-nothing-measures-whether-a-session-follows-the-rules-and.md):
    an eval harness for the rules and lessons;
  - [FM-097](FM-097-find-drift-does-not-audit-the-owner-s-rulings-whether-each.md): `/find-drift` audits the rulings;
  - [FM-098](FM-098-nothing-leads-from-a-commit-to-the-conversation-behind-it.md):
    a decision on linking a commit to the conversation behind it.
- **Its allocation:** v0.20.3's allocation is the Owner's, and this chapter waits for it (`next: wait`). It asks
  nothing of its own: the allocation's ask will name it, once it is filed.

## Why

A theme that is a tracker never ends, which is the very case FM-086 closes. A chapter per release keeps the theme
alive and each step of it finishable: the chapter ships when its parts do, and the next chapter names it.
