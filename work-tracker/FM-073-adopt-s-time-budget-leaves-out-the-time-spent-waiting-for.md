# FM-073 — ADOPT's time budget leaves out the time spent waiting for the person

## What is true now

**Filed 2026-10-27, Proposed, from a field report from a Windows working copy, on v0.20.1.** It comes after v0.20.2, on the Owner's allocation as relayed by the Auditor; nothing is built.

The adoption notes' time budget leaves out the time spent waiting for the person.

## Why

A trial that waits on the person for an approval or an answer runs over a budget that never counted the wait, and its report reads as slow.

## Done when

- `ADOPT.md`, `ADOPT.svn.md` and their German twins count the time spent waiting for the person apart from the agent's own time, and the report says both.
