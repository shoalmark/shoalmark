## The variants and what they mean on the chart (K)

The source is de.wikipedia, *Pricke*. For the port side: *"an deren oberen Ende unten zusammengebundene Zweige … (stumpfe
Form)"*. For the starboard side: *"oben zusammengebundene Zweige, die unten auseinandergebogen sind (spitze Form)"*. And
*"Es werden überwiegend Backbordpricken verwendet"*.

| Id | What is drawn | Chart meaning, one line |
|---|---|---|
| `a` | A stake, and seven twigs lashed at its head, fanned over ±36°. The twigs are equal in length, so their tips lie on one arc. They taper from 2 units to 0.3 | ***Besen nach oben*** marks the **port** side (Backbord, the red side). Its twigs are bound at their lower end and splay upward (the blunt form). It is the Pricke used most |
| `b` | The same crown turned down, lashed at the head of a stake that runs through it | ***Besen nach unten*** marks the **starboard** side (Steuerbord, the green side). Its twigs are bound at the top and bent apart below (the pointed form) |
| `c` | `a`'s crown, one unit smaller, on a stake that stands in a short water line of three crests | No chart meaning of its own: it is `a` where it stands |
| `d` | `a` cut for 16 px on a 16-unit grid. Four strokes (two at 45°, two at 1:2) meet in a 4-px neck on a 2-px stake. Every pixel is either full or empty | The browser tab's `a` |

Each SVG is under 1.1 KB and uses one colour, `currentColor`. In the header, `a`–`c` sit at 28 px, the same 1.4rem as
the GtM's B1, where one unit is one pixel. `d` sits at 32 px, twice its grid, so that its pixels stay square.

## What the pixels show (E)

**At 16 px** (`renders/sheet-16px-x8.png`; the true size is `renders/sheet-16px.png`):

- **`a`, `b` and `c` do not survive 16 px.** They are drawn on a 28-unit grid.
  - Each has 41–50 grey levels and 52–57 partly inked pixels.
  - The stake falls on 1.14 px and renders grey: two columns of 108 in `a` and `b`, four (207, 110, 110, 207) in `c`.
  - No row of the crown shows more than two separate strokes; in `b` and `c`, none shows more than one.
  - This is what `d` is for.
- **`d` has two values only, black and white, with no partly inked pixel.**
  - The stake is 2 px of full ink.
  - The crown's widest row shows four separate strokes.
  - It looks the same on white and on black.
- **B1, for reference on the same sheet:** four grey levels and 22 partly inked pixels (the edges of the balls). Its
  stake is full ink.

**In the header** (`renders/lockups.png` shows every variant at the same scale; the full pages are
`renders/header-{a,b,c,d}-{light,dark}-{1x,2x}.png`):

- **The stake of `a`, `b` and `c` is exactly 2 px of full ink at 1x, and 4 px at 2x.** The full-height stems of the
  wordmark (*h*, *l*, *k*) are 2 px plus a soft edge at 1x, and 4 plus edges at 2x. The mark and the name have the same
  weight.
- **The crown at 1x:**
  - `a` shows at most 6 of its 7 twigs apart on any one row; `b` shows 5, and `c` shows 6.
  - At 2x, all three show all 7 apart.
  - So at 1x the crown has a soft top, and at 2x every twig is drawn.
- **Height against the name:**
  - The wordmark's ink runs from row 18 (the top of the ascenders) to row 31 (the baseline).
  - `a`'s ink at 50 % or more runs from row 15 to row 38. The crown stands 3 px above the ascenders, and the stake's
    foot 7 px below the baseline.
  - `b`'s pointed top starts at row 13.
- **`d` in the header** is 32 × 32 px, the largest of the four. Its wider slot pushes the name 4 px to the right.
- **Dark:** the theme draws the logo in its foreground grey, 189 of 255, and the name in white, 255. This holds for all
  four variants and for B1 alike. On the dark ground, the mark is dimmer than the name because of the theme, not the
  drawing.

**Readings, not measurements (I):**

- None of the four has the dot of an *i* or the crossbar of a †.
- `b`'s crown reads as an arrowhead at 1x and at 2x, and the pointed form is the chart's own.
- `a` and `c` read as a fan, or a broom, on a stake. The Owner has accepted the broom.
- `c`'s water line is a ripple 3 px tall at 1x (rows 36–38, six dark pixels a row), and turns into speckle at 16 px.

## The one I would put in front of the Owner first — a proposal

**`a` in the header, with `d` as its tab.**

- **`a` is the Pricke most used on the Watt.** A Wadden sailor sees it most, so it is *the* Pricke, not one of two.
- **It holds up in the header.** Its stake is full ink at the name's own weight. Its seven twigs are separate at 2x and
  form a fan at 1x. It rises above the ascenders, so it stands as one figure, taller than the name, beside it.
- **The other two cost something.** `b` is just as true, but it reads as an arrowhead. `c` adds a second element that
  is a 3-px ripple at 1x and speckle at 16 px.
- **`d` is `a`'s own cut.** It is the only one of the four that is crisp at 16 px.

**Beside B1** (`renders/pair-B1-a.png`, the same page and slot, light and dark, 1x and 2x):

- At 1x, B1's lower ball fuses into its stake: its centre column is ink without a break from row 21 to row 39. This
  is the GtM's counterfact, and it is visible here.
- `a`'s crown at 1x has a soft top, and B1 is the crisper figure at 16 px.
- Whether either one is attractive is the Owner's eye to judge, not a gate this note can run.

## 2026-09-24 — the site wears `d`, the Owner's trial

The Owner locked `a` in the header with `d` as the tab (≈ 00:55), then asked to try **`d` alone, beside the wordmark
in IBM Plex Mono** (*"let's try d alone as proposed with the mono, this could be stellar"*, ≈ 01:05, relayed). The
branch `fm/006-the-site-wears-the-pricke` builds that. `site-header-a-vs-d-2x.png` is what he decides on.

- **The mark is inlined, not linked.** The theme's `logo` key renders an `<img>`, and inside an `<img>` the SVG's
  `currentColor` is black. That was built and rendered: black on the dark header. The mark is therefore the theme's
  own logo icon (`custom_dir = "overrides"`, `.icons/shoalmark/pricke.svg`, `icon.logo`), which takes the scheme's
  colour: 33 on light, 189 on dark.
- **It is hard-edged.** It uses `shape-rendering="crispEdges"` and is shown at 16 px, a whole multiple of its grid, not
  at the theme's 1.2rem (1.5×). The built header's mark box has exactly two grey levels at 1× and at 2×, light and dark:
  every stroke edge is a hard pixel edge.
- **The scale is ×1, 16 px.** The Mono wordmark's full-height stems are 2 px at 1× and 3–4 px at 2×, at 50 % ink.
  `d`'s stake at ×1 is 2 px and 4 px, the same weight. At ×2 (32 px) it would be 4 px and 8 px, twice the stems.
  The comparison shows both.
- **The type, as the browser reports it:** the wordmark is `"IBM Plex Mono"` 500 at 18px, and the page text is
  `"IBM Plex Sans"`; both are loaded. The theme loads its code font at 400 and 700 only, so
  `stylesheets/shoalmark.css` fetches Plex Mono 500 for the wordmark. That file changes nothing else except the logo's
  size.
- **The tab** is `docs/assets/favicon.svg`: `d`'s pixels, ink `#1a1a1a`, white when the browser is dark.
  `site-tab-16px-x8.png` shows it read back from the built `<link rel="icon">` on a light tab and a dark one. The theme
  emits one icon link, and no PNG is added.
- **Dark mode** is the theme's palette: a light and a dark scheme, following the system, with the toggle. The renders
  are the unmodified built page. The browser is told to prefer light or dark, and the theme picks the scheme itself.
- `site_description` stays English on both pages. The theme has one per site, and a German one per page would be
  new copy, which is not for tonight.

`python3 pricke.py site site` makes the five `site-*` renders and prints the probe. `measure` prints the header's
